Features

Built for the Complexity of Modern Infrastructure

From IoT edge nodes to Kubernetes clusters, NodeAssure adapts to your topology and gives you the assurance you need to operate with confidence.

Real-Time Node Monitoring

Continuous health checks across all nodes with millisecond-resolution telemetry. Know the exact state of your infrastructure at any moment.

Intelligent Alerting

Context-aware alerts that cut through the noise. Route incidents to the right team via Slack, PagerDuty, email, or custom webhooks — instantly.

Distributed Topology Mapping

Automatically discover and visualize node relationships across multi-region, multi-cloud, and hybrid environments. No manual configuration required.

Predictive Health Analytics

Machine-learning models trained on infrastructure failure patterns surface degradation signals before they become outages — hours ahead of impact.

Unified Dashboard

A single pane of glass for your entire infrastructure. Customizable views, team-based access controls, and one-click drill-downs into any node.

Compliance & Audit Trails

Immutable logs and compliance-ready reports for SOC 2, ISO 27001, and custom frameworks. Evidence collection is automated — audits are never a scramble.

How It Works

Up and Running in Minutes

No agents to wrangle. No complex configuration. NodeAssure integrates with your existing infrastructure stack in three simple steps.

01

Connect Your Infrastructure

Deploy the NodeAssure collector via a single Docker container, Helm chart, or cloud-init script. Auto-discovery finds every node in your environment within minutes.

02

Define Your Assurance Rules

Use our policy-as-code framework to specify what "healthy" means for each node type. Start from community templates or build your own in minutes.

03

Get Assured

NodeAssure continuously validates your rules, surfaces anomalies, and alerts your team the moment something drifts from the expected state.
